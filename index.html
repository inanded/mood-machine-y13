The Mood Machine

Over eleven sessions you will build the Mood Machine: an AI that reads the mood on a cartoon face. You will train it, test it, find out how it learns, and try to make it better. Every session has short videos for each part and, from Session 2, a starter notebook.

11 sessionsPython 3.13 + PyTorchJupyter Notebook

The Sessions

Session 1

Can a Computer Read a Face?

Rules or examples, pictures as numbers, and three ways a machine can learn.
Session 2

Set Up and Tensors

Start Jupyter, make your first tensors, paint a tiny picture and meet the faces.
Session 3

Inside a Neuron

Weights, bias and ReLU, worked through one neuron at a time, then a whole layer.
Session 4

The Ball Drop

Collect your own bounce data, and score a guessed line with the mean squared error.
Session 5

Gradient Descent

Let the computer walk downhill to the best line, and see what the learning rate does.
Session 6

The Faces Become Data

Seeds, a Dataset of 15,000 faces, training and test sets, batches and epochs.
Session 7

Build MoodNet

Flatten, three layers, 820,874 numbers to learn, and an untrained guess.
Session 8

Train the Mood Machine

Cross-entropy, the Adam optimiser and the training loop. Train it from 10% to over 80%.
Session 9

Back Propagation by Hand

Do what loss.backward() does, on a tiny network, and check every step.
Session 10

Catch It Out

New faces, faces you draw yourself, upside-down faces, and emotion AI and the law.
Session 11

The Mood Machine Challenge

Overfitting, then a fair-test competition to train the best Mood Machine.

Before Session 2

You need two files in a folder called mood-machine in your Documents. The Setup page shows every step with screenshots.

emoji_faces.py Start Jupyter.bat

Each session also has a starter notebook to download at the top of its page. Save it into the same mood-machine folder.

Writing Code Yourself

In Sessions 5, 8 and 11 you write code in an empty cell, following an outline in comments. Each of these tasks has hints on the page, opened one at a time, and a CHECK cell that tells you whether your code works. The PyTorch cheat sheet has every pattern in one place.

Going Further

Finished the unit and want more? The Olympiad in AI page introduces the UK and International AI Olympiads, and the next ideas: a network that sees shapes, augmentation, and better ways to score a model.

How Each Session Works

Work down the page at your own pace. Your teacher will stop the class at some points to discuss or check answers together.

The ideas the rest of the unit builds on.

Think

Stretch

Optional, harder work for when you have finished.

Check your understanding

Questions that ask you to explain an idea in full, in writing. Write your answer first, then open the model answer and compare.

Describe regression methods in machine learning Sessions 4 and 5
